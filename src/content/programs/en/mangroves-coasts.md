## Purpose

Restore mangrove functions and support coastal protection through joint management with community groups. Activities respond to site conditions, including erosion, tidal inundation, and conditions for plant growth.

## Main activities

- Site assessment and restoration planning.
- Mangrove nurseries, planting, replacement planting, and maintenance.
- Monitoring plant growth, sedimentation, and shoreline change.
- Supporting management groups and enterprises compatible with conservation.

## Locations and implementation

Programs operate in Buruk Bakul, Kelapa Pati, and Sepahat in Bengkalis Regency, and Tanjung Kuras in Siak Regency. Mangrove planting also takes place with communities in Teluk Piyai Pesisir, Rokan Hilir Regency. Local groups participate in nursery management, maintenance, and monitoring.

## Results and monitoring

Records cover planting locations and areas, seedling numbers and species, and community participation. Plant monitoring assesses survival and growth. Seedlings planted and surviving trees are reported separately.
