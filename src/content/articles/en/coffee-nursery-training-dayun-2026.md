Liberica coffee nursery training took place at the Dayun Village Office in Siak Regency on 25 July 2026. Participants included 23 men and 20 women. Of the 43 participants, 17 were young people: 10 men and 7 women.

T. Said Eka Nusirhan from Endemic and Ainul Azizah from Yayasan Gambut delivered the training. The activity formed part of the SPM–CLS program with Global Environment Centre, supporting peatland management and community livelihoods.
