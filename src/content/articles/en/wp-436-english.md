<p>For northern Sumatra, the July–September outlook indicated potentially lower rainfall than in the south, while conditions were expected to remain broadly normal and stable. Temperatures were also expected to be near normal. Across most of Kalimantan, rainfall was forecast to remain near normal, while southern Kalimantan was expected to be warmer than the north.</p>
