<p><strong>Pekanbaru, 6 December 2025</strong> — The Faculty of Agriculture, University of Riau, once again held <strong>the 3rd National Seminar on Sustainable Agriculture (SENAPELAN)</strong> with the theme <em>“Innovation and Collaboration to Improve the Competitiveness of Indonesian Coconuts: Challenges and Opportunities in the Era of Globalization and Climate Change.”</em> The event brought together national experts, academics, researchers, and industry players who have expertise in the field of agriculture and coconut commodities, including a representative of Yayasan Gambut.</p>
<p><strong>Opening and Welcome by the Dean of FP UNRI</strong></p>
<p><img src="https://yayasangambut.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/YayasanGambut-Dekan-FAPERTA-UR-1024x682.jpeg" alt=""></p>
<p><em>Dr. Ahmad Rifai, S.P., M.P., Dean of the Faculty of Agriculture at UNRI, delivering remarks</em></p>
<p>The Dean of the Faculty of Agriculture, University of Riau, <strong>Dr. Ahmad Rifai, S.P., M.P.</strong>, officially opened the seminar and emphasized that coconuts are an important commodity with great potential for further development through research, innovation, and multi-party collaboration. He stressed the need for synergy between the government, universities, the business world, and the community to strengthen Indonesia's position as the world's largest coconut producer.</p>
<p><strong>Presentation by the Director of Yayasan Gambut</strong></p>
<p><img src="https://yayasangambut.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/yayasangambut-Direktur-YG-1024x682.jpeg" alt=""></p>
<p><em>Presentation of materials by Mulyadi, S.P. (Director of Yayasan Gambut)</em></p>
<p>The presentation session by the speaker was led by <strong>Dr. Isna Rahma Dini, S.Pi., M.Si.</strong>, featuring experts from various fields, including agroforestry, agronomy, to coconut marketing.</p>
<p>Mulyadi, S.P., Director of Yayasan Gambut, presented the <strong>Coconut–Coffee Agroforestry Model on Peat Ecosystems</strong> as a strategic approach to supporting <strong>low-emission agriculture</strong>, <strong>landscape resilience</strong>, as well as <strong>improvement of community welfare</strong> in peatland areas. He presented the profile of Yayasan Gambut, the main challenges, as well as the social, economic, and environmental benefits of <strong>the Agroforestry Coconut-Coffee model.</strong></p>
<p>The Director explained that this agroforestry model combines <strong>coconut as the main shade tree</strong> and <strong>Liberica coffee as an adaptive peat commodity</strong>. This system aims to:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Maximize land use without opening new land</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Maintain the ecological function of peat while remaining productive</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Reduce the risk of land fires and degradation</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Realize low-emission agriculture</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Provide two sources of income for farmers (coconut and coffee)</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>It was stated that Liberica coffee is very suitable for peatland because:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Resistant to acidic soil and wet conditions</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Has a larger plant and fruit size</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Has been proven to be productive on lowland peat</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Research Dissemination Session</strong></p>
<p>In addition to the main presentation, SENAPELAN 2025 also held a special session for the presentation of research results related to coconut and sustainable agriculture, with contributions from researchers across different groups, including lecturers, students, teachers, and general researchers.</p>
<p>The seminar was closed with the hope that this activity can become a regular agenda that can bridge science, public policy, and technology implementation in the field, so that it can encourage the development of innovative, competitive, and sustainable national coconut agriculture.</p>
