Yayasan Gambut held Liberica coffee nursery training at the Pedekik Village Office in Bengkalis Regency on 17 June 2026. The session involved 26 men and 10 women, with Raja Alpian as the trainer.

The training formed part of Sustainable Peatland Management and Community Livelihood Strengthening (SPM–CLS), running from May 2026 to February 2027 with Global Environment Centre. Coffee nurseries are one component supporting agroforestry and community livelihoods on peatlands.
