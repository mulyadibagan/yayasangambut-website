A canal block was built in Temiang Village, Bandar Laksamana District, Bengkalis Regency, on 20 September 2026. Construction involved one woman from the Makmur Jaya women’s farming group and four men from the local community.

Work included installing wooden piles, compacting soil, and cement work on the upper section. The activity formed part of SPM–CLS with Global Environment Centre.

The structure is intended to retain water in peatlands. Monitoring its condition and water levels is needed to assess its performance after construction.
