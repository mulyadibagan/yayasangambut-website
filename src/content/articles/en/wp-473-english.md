<p>A webinar on community peatland management in the Giam Siak Kecil and Kerumutan landscapes, Riau Province, was held on Friday, 3 September 2021.</p>
<p>The event was organised by Yayasan Gambut with Global Environment Centre (GEC) and the Riau Provincial Environment and Forestry Office. Funding was provided by the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) through the TAKE-SMPEM programme.</p>
<p>Moderator: Fadil Nandila (KAWALI Riau Hijau).</p>
<p>Opening remarks: Dr. Ir. Mamun Murod, MM, MH, Head of the Riau Provincial Environment and Forestry Office, and Faizal Parish, Director of Global Environment Centre.</p>
<p>Presentations: Mulyadi (Yayasan Gambut), Makruf Siregar (Riau Provincial SMPEI Programme Coordinator), Tuti Sarinem (Makmur Jaya Women Farmers’ Group, Temiang) and Samrizal (Madu Ku Bisa Group, Penampi).</p>
